Copywriting Project 02
Email Copywriting · SaaS

A better first hello for a new user.

A welcome email concept for FlowPilot, a fictional productivity tool designed to help people organise tasks and keep their work moving.

01 — The Brief

Turn a sign-up into a first step.

A welcome email should do more than say thank you. It should reassure a new user, explain what to do next, and make the first interaction feel simple.

02 — The Copy

The welcome email

The following is the proposed email copy, displayed in a simple inbox-inspired layout.

From: FlowPilot Team
Subject: Your next clear step starts here
Preview text: Let's get your workspace ready.
flowpilot.

Welcome aboard.
Let's make work flow.

Hi there,

Welcome to FlowPilot! We're glad you're here. When your tasks, notes and priorities are scattered, knowing where to start can feel harder than the work itself.

FlowPilot gives you one place to organise what matters and see your next steps more clearly.

Ready to get started?

Create your first task, choose a priority, and give yourself a clearer starting point for the day.

Set Up Your Workspace →

No need to organise everything at once. Start with one task and build from there.

Here's to making progress feel a little simpler.

The FlowPilot Team

You're receiving this sample as a portfolio concept. FlowPilot is a fictional brand.
03 — The Thinking

Why this email works

01 / REASSURE

Start with empathy

Recognises a common organisation challenge before introducing the product's value.

02 / SIMPLIFY

One clear action

Guides the reader toward one practical first step instead of listing every feature.

03 / ENCOURAGE

Keep the pressure low

Friendly, straightforward language makes getting started feel manageable.

Portfolio note: This is an independent speculative copywriting project created for portfolio demonstration. FlowPilot is fictional; this work was not commissioned by or affiliated with a real client.
