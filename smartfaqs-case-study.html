Project overview

A small project with a clear purpose.

Understanding a product, identifying its value, and expressing that value in simple, useful language.

✦ Industry

Artificial intelligence and SaaS products.

✎ Project type

Independent landing-page copywriting concept.

◎ Deliverables

Hero copy, feature descriptions, CTAs, and messaging rationale.

01 / The challenge

Technology is useful only when people understand its value.

AI products can sound complicated when their messaging focuses on technology instead of what people can accomplish with it.

For this concept, I focused on communicating the value of asking questions about documents, finding relevant information, and checking source passages behind AI-generated answers.

The goal was to make the messaging clearer without inventing product capabilities or promising unverified results.

02 / The main message

A headline built around the user's problem.

Rather than leading with technical terminology, the proposed headline focuses on finding information.

Proposed landing-page headline

Your documents have answers. Make them easier to find.

Turn complex documents into a knowledge experience people can actually use. Upload your information, ask questions in natural language, and get AI-generated answers with citations to source passages.

04 / My writing decisions

From describing features to communicating value.

Here is the thinking behind the proposed messaging.

Feature-focused

AI can answer questions about documents.

Benefit-focused

Find answers without manually searching through lengthy documents.

Feature-focused

The answers include citations.

Benefit-focused

See the source passages behind an answer and check the information for yourself.

Feature-focused

The platform offers an AI voice agent.

Benefit-focused

Explore information through a voice-based AI experience.

05 / The strategy

Why I chose this approach.

Effective copywriting starts with understanding the reader, not just writing attractive sentences.

01

Lead with a recognizable problem

The headline connects with people who need to find information in documents.

02

Use straightforward language

Clear sentences make the product easier to understand without unnecessary technical jargon.

03

Highlight a meaningful differentiator

Source citations provide a concrete reason to pay attention to the evidence-based approach.

04

Give the reader a next step

A direct call to action invites interested readers to explore the product further.

Good copy makes complex ideas easier to understand.

This project demonstrates my approach to product messaging, benefit-led writing, and communicating technical ideas clearly.
