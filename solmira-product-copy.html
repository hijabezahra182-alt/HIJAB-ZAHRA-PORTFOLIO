E-commerce · Product Copy

Make the product feel worth discovering.

A product-page copy concept for Solmira, a fictional skincare brand with a calm, nature-inspired identity. The goal is to make the product easy to understand without relying on exaggerated promises.

01 — The Product

Meet the Solmira Daily Moisturiser.

The concept uses simple, benefit-led language to help shoppers understand the imagined product and picture where it could fit into their routine.

02 — Copy Breakdown

Writing with a purpose.

Each element has a job: attract attention, explain the value, or help the shopper decide what to do next.

Headline

“A softer start to your day.”

Supporting description

“Meet your everyday moisturising ritual: a simple, considered step designed to leave your skincare routine feeling less complicated.”

Call to action

“Explore the copy” — used here as a portfolio navigation link. On a real product page, a purchase-focused CTA could be tested against the brand's actual shopping journey.

03 — The Strategy

Why this approach?

01 / CLARITY

Lead with the experience

The headline creates a mood while the description explains the product's role in the routine.

02 / TRUST

Avoid overpromising

No invented clinical results, ingredient claims, or promises about skin outcomes.

03 / FLOW

Make it scannable

Short paragraphs and concise benefit points help shoppers find the key information quickly.

Portfolio note: Solmira and this product are fictional. This is an independent speculative copywriting project, not work commissioned by or affiliated with a real brand. Product benefits are illustrative, not verified claims.
